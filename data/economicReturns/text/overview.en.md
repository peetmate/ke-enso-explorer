Understanding the economic impacts of adaptation investments in agriculture is vital for investment planning. This notebook provides a detailed financial analysis for agricultural projects focused on adaptation, crucial for assessing the viability and strategic value of such investments.

Use this tool to evaluate the returns on a potential adaptation investment through projected economic performance indicators, including the: **Internal Rate of Return (IRR)**, **Modified Internal Rate of Return(MIRR)**, **Net Present Value (NPV)**, and **Benefit-Cost Ratio (BCR)**.
