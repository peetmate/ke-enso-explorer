---
title: "Project Set-Up"
---

Agricultural projects can have different levels of production outcomes (in terms of production gains), while also helping in reducing climate impacts. The project set-up allows investors to gauge project cashflows and financial metrics by adjusting such parameters.

To begin, select target crops and locations. Then, specify project cost and duration. Finally, set-up the innovation impact on yields and climate hazard reduction and its annual rate of adoption.
