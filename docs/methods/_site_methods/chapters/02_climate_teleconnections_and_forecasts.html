4 Equatorial Ocean Teleconnections & Seasonal Forecasting

This chapter presents the physical oceanographic indices, dynamical model plumes, and statistical verification frameworks governing Kenyan seasonal climate predictability. It establishes the detrended Relative Oceanic Niño Index (RONI), Indian Ocean Dipole Mode Index (DMI), multi-model forecast plume ensembles (SINTEX-F, IRI), and Ranked Probability Skill Score (RPSS) cross-validation protocols.
