7 Agricultural Production & Economic Valuation

This chapter formalizes the agricultural production anomaly functions, historical crop yield detrending, and the county economic capital valuation engine. It documents KNBS National Agriculture Production Report (NAPR) extractions, HarvestStat historical yields, and the subcounty spatial weight apportionment model under Decision D62/D63 Beta review governance.

7.1 Figure 1.1: County Agricultural & Livestock Production Time Series (2019–2024)

Section in Platform: Section 1.1  |  Target Identifier: fig_1_1  |  Output Type: Chart

Policy / Scientific Question: How has crop and livestock production varied over recent statutory reporting years?

7.1.1 Scientific Purpose & Context

Quantify and communicate County Agricultural & Livestock Production Time Series (2019–2024) across Kenyan counties to inform anticipatory action and climate adaptation planning.

7.1.2 Mathematical Formulation & Algorithmic Approach

Baseline Reference Period: 1991–2020 WMO standard normal

Official agricultural and pastoral asset inventories from KNBS National Agriculture Production Report (2024/2025). County share computed relative to national 47-county totals.

Applies non-parametric or standardized metrics aligned with Decision D43 and D53 to avoid distributional distortion.

Governing Mathematical Formulations:

\[ \text{Share}_{c} = \frac{Y_{c}}{\sum_{i=1}^{47} Y_{i}} \times 100\% \]

7.1.3 Underlying Data Assets & Inputs

Provenance Key Dataset Title Spatial Resolution Temporal Span
knbs-napr KNBS National Agricultural Production Re kenya 2019 to 2024

Served Parquet Files in Platform: knbs_napr_county_production.parquet, knbs_napr_livestock.parquet, knbs_napr_livestock_products.parquet

7.1.4 Methodological Assumptions & Analytical Limitations

  • County aggregations rely on official polygon boundaries from UN OCHA COD-AB standard.
  • Sub-seasonal temporal variations within rolling seasons are smoothed over seasonal totals.
  • Data latency reflects standard publication cadences of underlying satellite and administrative surveys.

7.1.5 Controlled Vocabulary Keywords

KNBS NAPR • Crops • Livestock Headcount • Milk Production

7.2 Figure 1.2: Modeled Value of Production Portfolio (MapSPAM & GLW4)

Section in Platform: Section 1.2  |  Target Identifier: fig_1_2  |  Output Type: Chart

Policy / Scientific Question: What agricultural and livestock capital assets are at economic risk across the county?

7.2.1 Scientific Purpose & Context

Quantify and communicate Modeled Value of Production Portfolio (MapSPAM & GLW4) across Kenyan counties to inform anticipatory action and climate adaptation planning.

7.2.2 Mathematical Formulation & Algorithmic Approach

Baseline Reference Period: 1991–2020 WMO standard normal

Multi-tier economic capital valuation modeling crop Gross Value of Production (VOP) in constant Int$2015 / USD and livestock herd capital replacement value. Subcounty apportionment via landcover and rangeland weighting (Decision D62/D63 Beta review governance).

Applies non-parametric or standardized metrics aligned with Decision D43 and D53 to avoid distributional distortion.

Governing Mathematical Formulations:

\[ V_{c} = \sum_{k} Q_{c,k} \cdot P_{k}^{\text{Int\$}} \]

\[ V_{sc} = V_{c} \cdot w_{sc}, \quad \sum_{sc \in c} w_{sc} = 1.0 \]

7.6.2 Mathematical Formulation & Algorithmic Approach

Baseline Reference Period: 1991–2020 WMO standard normal

Pastoral terms-of-trade (ToT) computed as the ratio of live medium-sized goat (shoat) price to 1 kg dry maize grain. Spikes and collapses indexed against ENSO shock windows.

Applies non-parametric or standardized metrics aligned with Decision D43 and D53 to avoid distributional distortion.

\[ \text{ToT}_{m} = \frac{P_{\text{goat},m}}{P_{\text{maize},m}} \quad (\text{kg maize per goat}) \]
